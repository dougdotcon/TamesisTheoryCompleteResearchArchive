# Tamesis Research Timeline

This is the short path from the original Tamesis research to the current focus.

## 1. Tamesis origin

The first phase was the broad theoretical search: a unified picture of physics, information, and structure.

What mattered here:

- the original Tamesis framing
- the early kernel ideas
- the attempt to understand continuity, gravity, and emergence

## 2. TRI / TDTR

The second phase sharpened the project into a more precise statement:

- TRI: regime incompatibility
- TDTR: dynamics of transitions between regimes

This is where the archive stopped being only a unification attempt and became a framework for understanding why a single ToE-style model was the wrong shape for the problem.

## 3. Tamesis M_c v1

The current active nucleus is the experimental line around `Tamesis M_c v1`.

This is now the main place to focus when asking:

- what can be tested
- what can be compared with Bohr-level historical significance
- what survives against competing models and noise
