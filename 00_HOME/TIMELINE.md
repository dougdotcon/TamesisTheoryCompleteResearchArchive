# Tamesis Research Timeline

This is the short path from the original Tamesis research to the current focus.

## 1. Tamesis origin

The first phase was the broad theoretical search: a unified picture of physics, information, and structure.

What mattered here:

- the original Tamesis framing
- the early kernel ideas
- the attempt to understand continuity, gravity, and emergence

## 2. TRI / TDTR

The second phase sharpened the project into a more precise statement:

- TRI: regime incompatibility
- TDTR: dynamics of transitions between regimes

This is where the archive stopped being only a unification attempt and became a framework for understanding why a single ToE-style model was the wrong shape for the problem.

## 3. Tamesis M_c v1

The current research nucleus is the experimental line around `Tamesis M_c v1`.

This is now the main place to focus when asking:

- what can be tested
- what can be compared with Bohr-level historical significance
- what survives against competing models and noise

## 4. Computational freeze and physical handoff

On 2026-07-26 the exclusively computational phase was frozen.

- the model and protocol are reproducible and audit-ready;
- the A0/A1 hardware package is ready with limitations;
- hardware qualification has not started;
- there is no physical evidence for Tamesis;
- the project is paused pending real hardware and metrology.

The next stage is not another simulation version. It is Q0 hardware
qualification followed by real A0 particle characterization and blind A1
thermometry validation.

## How to read the archive now

The archive is not dead. It is layered.

- `01_TAMESIS_CORE` holds the current core path
- `90_LEGACY` keeps older branches and abandoned routes
- `00_HOME` gives the map
- `PROJECT_FREEZE.md` states exactly what is frozen and how work may resume
