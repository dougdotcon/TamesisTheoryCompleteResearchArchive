# Auditoria — TRI — Regime Incompatibility

Data: 2026-07-30  
Status: auditoria de registro CANON.

## Achados

TRI is a framework, not a proved no-go theorem. The claims depend on assumptions about discreteness, continuity, diffeomorphism and unitary evolution; a larger theory may evade them.

## Teste mínimo

Publish formal statements, domains, proof obligations and counterexamples.
