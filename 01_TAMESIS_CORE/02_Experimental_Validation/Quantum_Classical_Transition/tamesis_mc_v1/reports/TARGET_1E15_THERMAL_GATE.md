# Target 1e-15 kg: thermal gate

The extracted literature gives a conservative thermal rule:

- for micrometer-size superpositions in diamond, blackbody radiation becomes a
  considerable decoherence channel above `~4 K`.

Because the current target is around `50 μm`, treat `4 K` as an upper gate, not
as a comfort zone.

## Thermal classes

- `T <= 1 K`: low blackbody risk
- `1 K < T <= 4 K`: borderline
- `T > 4 K`: blackbody dominant

## Current decision

To keep the `1e-15 kg` target Bohr-level, the experiment should be designed
well below `4 K`, ideally around `1 K` or colder.
