# Optimal experimental design

The Pareto search reports D-, E-, minimum-singular-value, predictive discrimination, robustness and cost components separately. The recommended minimum is D5 with five masses (0.8, 1.0, 1.05, 1.8894, 2.0)Mc, three times (0.01, 0.1, 1.0 s), three pressures (1e-13, 1e-11, 1e-9 Pa), and three environmental controls.
