# Blind calibration of GeV nanodiamond thermometry at 5–20 K

## Project summary

We seek an experimental collaboration to determine whether the optical response
of an individually identified germanium-vacancy (GeV) diamond particle can
serve as a calibrated thermometer between 5 K and 20 K.

The immediate project is a stationary metrology experiment. It does not require
levitation and does not test a collapse model or a new law of physics.

## Scientific question

Can a particle-specific spectral model reconstruct hidden reference
temperatures across 5–20 K with quantified uncertainty, repeatability, and an
acceptable optical self-heating budget?

## Proposed work

**Q0 — hardware qualification**

Document the cryostat, reference and stage thermometers, thermal controller,
laser, power meter, wavelength reference, spectrometer, detector, magnetic
field sensors, timing, raw-data storage, and calibration traceability.

**A0 — pilot particle characterization**

For each persistently identified particle, acquire raw dark/background spectra,
low-power spectra, randomized power sweeps, return-to-low-power checks,
stability time series, saturation behavior, spectral diffusion, and available
field/strain cross-sensitivity measurements.

**A1 — blind stationary calibration**

Only after Q0 and A0 pass: perform ascending, descending, and randomized
temperature blocks from 5 K to 20 K. The analysis team fits the development
data, freezes the model, predicts hidden validation temperatures, and receives
the reference-temperature reveal only after prediction hashes are locked.

## Requested collaboration

The experimental partner provides equipment, sample access or fabrication,
operation, calibration evidence, and raw data. Douglas H. M. Fulber coordinates
the protocol, provenance, reproducibility, and blind analysis. Roles and
authorship are agreed before data acquisition.

The smallest useful first engagement is:

1. a 30–45 minute technical feasibility call;
2. completion of a short Q0 inventory;
3. if viable, one A0 pilot on one identifiable GeV particle.

## Deliverables

- auditable hardware and calibration inventory;
- preserved raw spectra and telemetry;
- A0 decision: `advance_to_A1`, `repeat_A0`, `reject_candidate`, or
  `inconclusive`;
- if A1 proceeds, a blind thermometry validation with locked predictions;
- a publishable metrology result whether the candidate succeeds or fails.
