# Audit — Kernel v3, Russian version

**Status:** needs_data  
**Class:** speculative cosmological model; translated record (S0/M)

## Verdict

The discrete tensor network and logistic saturation are modelling assumptions. They do not prove finite curvature for every Einstein solution or establish a consistent replacement for general relativity. The proposed frequency has no complete derivation or detectability calculation.

## Required tests

Provide covariant effective equations, conservation and stability analyses, the `ΛCDM` limit, and a comparison with gravitational-wave constraints. State the assumptions behind the Bekenstein bound.
